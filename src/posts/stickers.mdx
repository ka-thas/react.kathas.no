I absolutely love stickers! They just stuck with me after LittleBigPlanet taught me to put stickers on any and all surfaces. The ones on iOS feel more personal, because they are from moments I've experienced. The surrounding context of an image is stripped away, so only a select few people know where and what happened. They're also such a blast to drop into the group chat for the first time.

I hope you're turned into one of my stickers, friend.
