I absolutely love stickers! They just stuck with me after Little Big Planet taught me to put stickers on any and all surfaces. The ones on iOS feel more personal, in my opinion, because they are from situations you've experienced. You strip away the surrounding context of an image, so only a select few people know where and what happened. They're also such a blast to drop into the group chat for the first time.

I hope you become one of my stickers, friend.
