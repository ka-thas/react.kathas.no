Oh so you want to build a website? Fine, here’s your guide.

✎﹏﹏﹏﹏

Scoping! Before you begin, you should scope out your ambitions and your current abilities. “First make it exist, then make it pretty” → Build it iteratively.

If you just need a link-tree for your social medias and other links, you can go with a vanilla html-css-js website; the holy trinity that all web-dev boils down to. I would actually recommend starting with this anyways, just to see how everything works. This can even be hosted using github pages, which I happen to have [another guide](/blog/how-to-slenge-opp-en-nettside) on.

Feel free to DM or email me if you have any questions! 

Now if you’re planning to make an insanely feature-rich website, Next.js might be the way to go. Although I will say that it is insanely over-kill for most personal websites. Vite React is lightweight, but you need to wire up your own routing and backend (if needed).

My tech stack (as of writing):

- Vite + React
- Tailwind CSS
- Hosted on Vercel
- Domain bought at domeneshop

Other frameworks to consider:

- Tanstack
- Next.js with a sanity CMS

### Other general tips

Do your own quick research! The web-dev landscape is constantly changing, and the meta is due to change.

Also, try to pour in as much personality into the website as possible. This is *your* space. We do love the human touch and some human errors! Let’s bring back the old internet ⋆˚✿˖°

Refuse to let AI be the ceiling of what you can create. If you’re nothing without AI, then you shouldn’t have it

Use git from day one
